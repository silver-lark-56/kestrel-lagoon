**KESTREL LAGOON** — an open-world crime story in an original sun-soaked lagoon city, built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty. Three years gone for a job that went sideways. Rook is back in Kestrel Lagoon — and the Dredgers own the water now.

Drive and fight across a living 3D city (causeway bridge, marina, stilt houses, airstrip, neon quay) in three.js. 8 story jobs with named contacts and payouts, a 1–5 star wanted system with named KLPD pursuit units, helicopter + spotlight, roadblocks and search zones, and **LIVE CREW ORDERS**: three recruitable crew — Mara the driver, Ivo the hacker, Sana the lookout — who walk beside you, take orders mid-mission (FOLLOW / HOLD / ATTACK / DISTRACT), and run signature abilities (getaway car delivery, camera blackout, enemy marking). Around the story: 3 street races, taxi gigs, 2 income properties, 20 Gull Caches, a gun shop, respray garage, safehouse saves, 3 procedural radio stations, and a full day/night cycle. All textures are painted procedurally on canvas and all sound is synthesized WebAudio — the game runs fully offline, no network.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```

Play: WASD move/drive, mouse aim, click shoot, F steal/exit car, E interact, M map & jobs, C crew order, X crew ability, T pick crew, Q radio, Esc pause. Staged screenshots: `?shot=cover|title|combat|map&seed=1`. Scripted self-test: `?selftest=1` (results on `window.__selftest`).
